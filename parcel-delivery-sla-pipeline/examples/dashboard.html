Metric definitions

On-time delivery: Delivered at or before the promised UTC timestamp. The denominator includes delivered parcels only.

Open overdue: Not delivered and the promise timestamp is earlier than the report reference date.

Exception history: Any exception scan, even if delivery later succeeded.

Average delivery time: 54.51 hours among delivered parcels.
